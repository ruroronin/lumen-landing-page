**Last updated: [26/10/2026]**

### Who we are

Lumen is operated by Ricardo Ramos, based in Portugal.

For anything in this policy, contact **<ricardo@uselumen.dev>**.

### What we collect

If you join the waitlist, we collect:

- Your **email address**
- The **date** you signed up

That's all. We don't use analytics or tracking cookies on this site, and we
don't collect anything else about you.

### Why we collect it

To send you:

- An invite when the Lumen alpha opens
- A short update on what's being built, roughly every two weeks

Nothing else. We don't sell your address, share it with advertisers, or pass it
to third parties for their own purposes.

### Legal basis

Consent. You gave it by entering your address and submitting the form, and you
can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing. Every email we send includes an
unsubscribe link.

### Who processes it

Your email address is stored and the messages are sent by **Kit** (Kit.com,
formerly ConvertKit), our email service provider, acting on our instructions.

Kit is based in the United States, so your data is transferred outside the EEA.
That transfer relies on the standard contractual clauses in Kit's data
processing terms.

Kit's privacy policy: <https://kit.com/privacy>

### How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe, or until you ask us to delete it. If Lumen is
discontinued, the list is deleted.

### Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

- Give you a copy of the data we hold about you
- Correct it if it's wrong
- Delete it
- Export it in a portable format
- Stop using it, or restrict how we use it

Email **<ricardo@uselumen.dev>** and we'll action it. No charge, no hoops.

You can also complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority, the **Comissão
Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)**: <https://www.cnpd.pt>

### Cookies

This site doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies, so there's no cookie
banner. If that changes, this policy changes with it and you'll be asked first
where consent is required.

### Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and anyone on
the list is told by email.
